CD-ARCH1 [Non-Functional Requirement] SystemArchitecture: The Chemical Detector system is an autonomous mobile robot that searches for the source of a target chemical in an unknown environment. It carries a Vehicle with gas sensors, an obstacle detector, and an odometer, and is capable of moving with configurable linear velocity and turning to any of four directions (Left, Right, Back, Front) relative to its body. The system operates in a continuous sense-analyse-act loop until the chemical source is confirmed.

CD-ARCH2 [Non-Functional Requirement] ControllerArchitecture: The Chemical Detector is composed of three subsystems: (1) a sensing-and-actuation surface — the Vehicle — that exposes sensor events (gas, obstacle, odometer) and provides movement operations (move, randomWalk, shortRandomWalk); (2) a gas-analysis subsystem that classifies sensor readings and decides whether to keep searching, change direction towards a stronger signal, or stop because the chemical source has been found; and (3) a movement subsystem that performs random-walk search, follows direction commands, handles obstacle avoidance, and recovers when stuck. The two reasoning subsystems communicate only via the turn, stop, and resume events; the movement subsystem emits the flag event to the Vehicle when the chemical source is found.

CD-DM1 [Non-Functional Requirement] StatusEnum: The Status enumeration captures the outcome of a single gas-analysis cycle. It has exactly two values: noGas (current reading does not indicate the target chemical) and gasD (current reading indicates the target chemical is present).

CD-DM2 [Non-Functional Requirement] AngleEnum: The Angle enumeration represents a turn direction relative to the robot body. It has exactly four values: Left, Right, Back, Front. The movement subsystem uses Angle values as the operand of the move operation.

CD-DM3 [Non-Functional Requirement] LocEnum: The Loc enumeration represents the side of the robot on which an obstacle has been detected. It has exactly three values: left, right, front. The movement subsystem receives Loc values through the obstacle event and passes them to the changeDirection operation.

CD-DM4 [Non-Functional Requirement] ChemType: Chem is an opaque type representing the identity of a chemical species. Sensor readings carry a Chem value to identify which chemical the reading is for. Equality is the only operation needed on Chem.

CD-DM5 [Non-Functional Requirement] IntensityType: Intensity is a totally-ordered type representing the strength of a sensor reading. The system compares two intensities via the goreq function (see CD-Fn4). Comparison is the only operation needed on Intensity.

CD-DM6 [Non-Functional Requirement] GasSensorRecord: A GasSensor is a record with two fields: c (Chem, the chemical identity) and i (Intensity, the measured intensity for that chemical). The Vehicle's gas event carries a sequence of GasSensor values (Seq(GasSensor)) representing a multi-sensor reading at one point in time.

CD-DM7 [Non-Functional Requirement] GasSensorReading: A GasSensorReading is a sequence of GasSensor values produced by the Vehicle's gas event. The position in the sequence (1-based index) encodes which sensor produced the reading, and is mapped to a direction by the angle function. The gas-analysis subsystem stores the most recent reading in the gs state variable.

CD-Const1 [Non-Functional Requirement] IntensityThreshold: The constant thr (Intensity) is the intensity threshold for declaring a chemical source 'found'. When the peak intensity of the current gas reading is at or above thr, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions to its final state and the movement subsystem emits the flag event. The value is configured at system startup and does not change at run time.

CD-Const2 [Non-Functional Requirement] LinearVelocity: The constant lv (real) is the linear velocity (in arbitrary units) at which the Vehicle moves during normal travel. It is the first argument to every move(lv, a) call made by the movement subsystem. The value is configured at system startup.

CD-Const3 [Non-Functional Requirement] EvadeTime: The constant evadeTime (nat) is the duration the system waits inside the Avoiding state after issuing a changeDirection command. This gives the changeDirection action time to complete before the next behaviour fires.

CD-Const4 [Non-Functional Requirement] StuckPeriod: The constant stuckPeriod (nat) is the time-window threshold for the stuck-detection rule. If less time than stuckPeriod has elapsed since the first obstacle of the current evasion sequence, the robot is considered to be making progress and continues avoiding; otherwise the recovery behaviour activates.

CD-Const5 [Non-Functional Requirement] StuckDist: The constant stuckDist (real) is the distance threshold for stuck detection. If the robot has travelled more than stuckDist between the first and second obstacles of an evasion sequence (d1 - d0 > stuckDist), it is considered to have escaped; otherwise it is considered stuck and the recovery behaviour activates.

CD-Const6 [Non-Functional Requirement] OutPeriod: The constant outPeriod (nat) is the duration the robot spends in the GettingOut state executing a shortRandomWalk after stuck detection has fired. It bounds the recovery manoeuvre before the robot returns to normal navigation.

CD-Fn1 [Functional Requirement] AnalysisFunction: The analysis function takes a sequence of GasSensor readings and returns a Status: noGas if no reading indicates the target chemical, gasD otherwise. This is the primary classifier used by the gas-analysis subsystem to decide whether the Vehicle should keep moving or stop.

CD-Fn2 [Functional Requirement] IntensityFunction: The intensity function takes a non-empty sequence of GasSensor readings and returns the maximum intensity across the sequence. Precondition: the input sequence is non-empty. Postcondition: the result is greater than or equal to every i field in the input, and the result equals some sensor's i field.

CD-Fn3 [Functional Requirement] LocationFunction: The location function takes a non-empty sequence of GasSensor readings and returns the Angle of the sensor with the highest intensity. Precondition: the input sequence is non-empty. Postcondition: there exists an index x such that gs[x].i equals intensity(gs) and the result equals angle(x). Used to steer the robot towards the strongest signal.

CD-Fn4 [Functional Requirement] IntensityCompare: The goreq function (intensity-greater-or-equal) compares two Intensity values and returns true iff the first is at least as large as the second. It is the only ordering predicate needed on Intensity and is used in the threshold check (intensity(gs) >= thr) and inside the intensity function's correctness condition.

CD-Evt1 [Non-Functional Requirement] GasEvent: The gas event carries a value of type Seq(GasSensor) representing one multi-sensor gas reading. The Vehicle emits gas; the gas-analysis subsystem receives it and stores the sequence in the gs variable for the gas-analysis subsystem to process.

CD-Evt2 [Non-Functional Requirement] ObstacleEvent: The obstacle event carries a value of type Loc indicating the side of the robot on which an obstacle has been detected. The Vehicle emits obstacle; the movement subsystem receives it and stores the value in the l variable for the movement subsystem.

CD-Evt3 [Non-Functional Requirement] OdometerEvent: The odometer event carries a value of type real representing the current cumulative distance travelled by the Vehicle. The Vehicle emits odometer; the movement subsystem consumes the value into the d0 or d1 variable to support stuck detection.

CD-Evt4 [Non-Functional Requirement] TurnEvent: The turn event carries a value of type Angle indicating the direction the robot should face next. The gas-analysis subsystem emits turn (with the result of the location function); the movement subsystem consumes it to update the a variable and trigger movement transitions.

CD-Evt5 [Non-Functional Requirement] StopEvent: The stop signal event (no payload) is emitted by the gas-analysis subsystem when the chemical source has been confirmed (peak intensity is at or above thr). The movement subsystem consumes stop to transition the movement subsystem to the Found state.

CD-Evt6 [Non-Functional Requirement] ResumeEvent: The resume signal event (no payload) is emitted by the gas-analysis subsystem after analysing a no-gas reading. The movement subsystem consumes resume to return its movement subsystem to the Waiting state and continue searching.

CD-Evt7 [Non-Functional Requirement] FlagEvent: The flag signal event (no payload) is emitted by the movement subsystem upon entering the Found state. It signals to the Vehicle that the chemical source has been located so the platform may halt and report.

CD-OP1 [Design Constraint] OperationMove: The move(lv : real, a : Angle) operation is provided by the Vehicle. Calling move causes the Vehicle to move with linear velocity lv in direction a (relative to the robot body). The operation does not terminate on its own; subsequent move calls override the previous one. Calling move(0, Angle::Front) halts the Vehicle.

CD-OP2 [Design Constraint] OperationRandomWalk: The randomWalk() operation is provided by the Vehicle. It performs an unbounded random-walk search. The robot invokes randomWalk while in the Waiting state to keep moving between sensor readings.

CD-OP3 [Design Constraint] OperationShortRandomWalk: The shortRandomWalk() operation is provided by the Vehicle and runs for a bounded period of time. The robot invokes shortRandomWalk on entry to the GettingOut state to perform a recovery manoeuvre when stuck detection has fired.

CD-OP4 [Design Constraint] OperationChangeDirection: The changeDirection(l : Loc) operation is invoked from the Avoiding-state entry behaviour. Its specified behaviour is: if l == Loc::left then move(lv, Angle::Right); if l == Loc::right then move(lv, Angle::Left); if l == Loc::front then move(lv, Angle::Back). lv is provided to the operation as a real constant.

CD-GA-Var1 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_gs: The gas-analysis subsystem maintains a state variable gs of type Seq(GasSensor) initialised to the empty sequence. gs is overwritten on every gas-event reception (Reading -> Analysis transition) and read by the analysis, intensity, and location functions.

CD-GA-Var2 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_sts: The gas-analysis subsystem maintains a state variable sts of type Status. sts is assigned in the Analysis state entry action (sts = analysis(gs)) and used as a guard in the transitions leaving Analysis.

CD-GA-Var3 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_ins: The gas-analysis subsystem maintains a state variable ins of type Intensity. ins is assigned in the GasDetected state entry action (ins = intensity(gs)) and compared against thr in the transitions leaving GasDetected.

CD-GA-Var4 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_anl: The gas-analysis subsystem maintains a state variable anl of type Angle. anl is assigned during the GasDetected -> Reading transition (anl = location(gs)) and immediately sent over the turn event in the same transition action.

CD-MV-Var1 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_a: The movement subsystem maintains a state variable a of type Angle. a is overwritten every time the turn event is received (turn ? a) and read by the Going / TryingAgain entry actions to call move(lv, a).

CD-MV-Var2 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_d0: The movement subsystem maintains a state variable d0 of type real. d0 is assigned in the Avoiding state entry action (odometer ? d0) and used by stuck detection (d1 - d0 > stuckDist) to decide whether the robot is making progress.

CD-MV-Var3 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_d1: The movement subsystem maintains a state variable d1 of type real. d1 is assigned in the TryingAgain -> AvoidingAgain transition action (odometer ? d1) and used together with d0 by stuck detection.

CD-MV-Var4 [Non-Functional Requirement] Variable_l: The movement subsystem maintains a state variable l of type Loc. l is assigned on every obstacle event reception (obstacle ? l) and passed to changeDirection(l) in the Avoiding state entry action.

CD-MV-Clock1 [Non-Functional Requirement] Clock_T: The movement subsystem maintains a clock T used by stuck-detection. T is reset on the Going -> Avoiding transition and on the AvoidingAgain -> Avoiding transition. The predicate since(T) returns the elapsed time since the last reset and is compared against stuckPeriod in the behaviours leaving AvoidingAgain.

CD-GA-FR1 [Functional Requirement] GA_StateNoGas: NoGas is a state of the gas-analysis subsystem reached after analysis classifies a reading as not indicating the target chemical. NoGas has no entry, during, or exit actions. The only transition out of NoGas returns to Reading; the resume event is sent on the way in.

CD-GA-FR2 [Functional Requirement] GA_StateReading: Reading is the initial state of the gas-analysis subsystem: control transitions from the initial state to Reading on system start. Reading has no entry, during, or exit actions. The only transition out of Reading consumes a gas event and moves to Analysis.

CD-GA-FR3 [Functional Requirement] GA_StateAnalysis: Analysis is the state where the gas-analysis subsystem classifies the most recent reading. On entry, the analysis function is applied to gs and the result is assigned to sts. Analysis has two outgoing transitions, both guarded by sts and both leaving Analysis on the same step.

CD-GA-FR4 [Functional Requirement] GA_StateGasDetected: GasDetected is the state entered when analysis returned gasD. On entry, the intensity function is applied to gs and the result is assigned to ins. GasDetected has two outgoing transitions guarded by the comparison goreq(ins, thr): one to the final state j1 (above threshold; send stop), one back to Reading (below threshold; turn towards the strongest signal and continue).

CD-MV-FR1 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateWaiting: Waiting is the initial state of the movement subsystem. The during action is randomWalk(), so while no event is being processed the robot performs unbounded random-walk search. Waiting receives turn (-> Going), stop (-> Found), and resume (-> Waiting self-loop) events.

CD-MV-FR2 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateGoing: Going is the state where the robot moves with linear velocity lv in direction a. The entry action move(lv, a) starts the motion. Going self-loops on a new turn event (re-issues move with the new angle), transitions to Found on stop, to Avoiding on obstacle, and to Waiting on resume.

CD-MV-FR3 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateFound: Found is the state entered when the chemical source has been confirmed. The entry action sends the flag event to the Vehicle and immediately calls move(0, Angle::Front) to halt the platform. The only transition out of Found goes to the final state j1.

CD-MV-FR4 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateAvoiding: Avoiding is the state entered when an obstacle has been detected during normal travel. The entry action sequences three operations: odometer ? d0 (record current distance), changeDirection(l) (turn away from the obstacle), wait(evadeTime) (give the turn time to complete). Avoiding transitions to TryingAgain on turn, to Found on stop, and to Waiting on resume.

CD-MV-FR5 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateTryingAgain: TryingAgain is the state where the robot resumes movement after an avoidance manoeuvre. The entry action move(lv, a) restarts motion in the most recent direction a. TryingAgain self-loops on a new turn event, transitions to Found on stop, to Waiting on resume, and to AvoidingAgain on a fresh obstacle (with action odometer ? d1 to seed stuck detection).

CD-MV-FR6 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateAvoidingAgain: AvoidingAgain is the state entered after a second obstacle is hit during a single evasion sequence. It has no entry, during, or exit actions. AvoidingAgain has two guarded transitions: back to Avoiding when the robot is judged to be making progress (since(T) < stuckPeriod OR d1 - d0 > stuckDist), or to GettingOut when stuck (since(T) >= stuckPeriod AND d1 - d0 <= stuckDist). It also transitions to Found on stop and to Waiting on resume.

CD-MV-FR7 [Functional Requirement] MV_StateGettingOut: GettingOut is the recovery state entered when stuck detection has fired. The entry action sequences shortRandomWalk() (a terminating random-walk) and wait(outPeriod) (bounded wait). GettingOut transitions to Going on turn, to Found on stop, and to Waiting on resume.

CD-GA-Beh1 [Functional Requirement] GA_Init: On system startup, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions from its initial state to Reading. No guard, no trigger, no action.

CD-GA-Beh2 [Functional Requirement] GA_Reading_to_Analysis: In Reading, on receiving the gas event with payload gs, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions to Analysis. Trigger: gas ? gs. No guard, no action.

CD-GA-Beh3 [Functional Requirement] GA_NoGas_to_Reading: In NoGas, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions back to Reading. No guard, no trigger, no action. This is an autonomous transition that allows the next reading to be collected.

CD-GA-Beh4 [Functional Requirement] GA_Analysis_to_NoGas: In Analysis, when the entry-action result sts equals Status::noGas, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions to NoGas. Guard: sts == Status::noGas. Action: send resume (so the movement subsystem returns to Waiting and continues searching). No trigger.

CD-GA-Beh5 [Functional Requirement] GA_Analysis_to_GasDetected: In Analysis, when the entry-action result sts equals Status::gasD, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions to GasDetected. Guard: sts == Status::gasD. No trigger, no action.

CD-GA-Beh6 [Functional Requirement] GA_GasDetected_to_Final: In GasDetected, when the peak intensity ins is at or above thr, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions to its final state j1. Guard: goreq(ins, thr). Action: send stop (so the movement subsystem moves to Found and halts).

CD-GA-Beh7 [Functional Requirement] GA_GasDetected_to_Reading: In GasDetected, when the peak intensity ins is below thr, the gas-analysis subsystem transitions back to Reading. Guard: NOT goreq(ins, thr). Action: anl = location(gs); send turn ! anl (so the movement subsystem steers towards the strongest signal).

CD-MV-Beh1 [Functional Requirement] MV_Init: On system startup, the movement subsystem transitions from its initial state to Waiting. No guard, no trigger, no action.

CD-MV-Beh2 [Functional Requirement] MV_Waiting_to_Going: In Waiting, on receiving the turn event with payload a, the movement subsystem transitions to Going. Trigger: turn ? a. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh3 [Functional Requirement] MV_Waiting_self_resume: In Waiting, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem self-loops back to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action. (Resume in Waiting is effectively a no-op but is permitted so cross-state resume transitions are uniformly handled.)

CD-MV-Beh4 [Functional Requirement] MV_Waiting_to_Found: In Waiting, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh5 [Functional Requirement] MV_Going_self_turn: In Going, on receiving the turn event with payload a, the movement subsystem self-loops back to Going. Trigger: turn ? a. No guard, no action. Re-entering Going re-fires the entry action move(lv, a) with the new angle.

CD-MV-Beh6 [Functional Requirement] MV_Going_to_Found: In Going, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh7 [Functional Requirement] MV_Going_to_Avoiding: In Going, on receiving the obstacle event with payload l, the movement subsystem transitions to Avoiding. Trigger: obstacle ? l. Action: reset clock T so stuck detection can measure the new evasion sequence. No guard.

CD-MV-Beh8 [Functional Requirement] MV_Going_to_Waiting: In Going, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem transitions to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh9 [Functional Requirement] MV_Found_to_Final: In Found, the movement subsystem transitions to its final state j1. No guard, no trigger, no action. Found's entry action has already sent the flag event and halted the platform.

CD-MV-Beh10 [Functional Requirement] MV_Avoiding_to_TryingAgain: In Avoiding, on receiving the turn event with payload a, the movement subsystem transitions to TryingAgain. Trigger: turn ? a. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh11 [Functional Requirement] MV_Avoiding_to_Found: In Avoiding, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh12 [Functional Requirement] MV_Avoiding_to_Waiting: In Avoiding, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem transitions to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh13 [Functional Requirement] MV_TryingAgain_self_turn: In TryingAgain, on receiving the turn event with payload a, the movement subsystem self-loops back to TryingAgain. Trigger: turn ? a. Re-entering TryingAgain re-fires move(lv, a) with the new angle.

CD-MV-Beh14 [Functional Requirement] MV_TryingAgain_to_Found: In TryingAgain, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh15 [Functional Requirement] MV_TryingAgain_to_Waiting: In TryingAgain, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem transitions to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh16 [Functional Requirement] MV_TryingAgain_to_AvoidingAgain: In TryingAgain, on receiving the obstacle event with payload l, the movement subsystem transitions to AvoidingAgain. Trigger: obstacle ? l. Action: odometer ? d1 (record current distance for stuck detection). No guard.

CD-MV-Beh17 [Functional Requirement] MV_AvoidingAgain_to_Avoiding: In AvoidingAgain, the movement subsystem transitions back to Avoiding when stuck detection judges the robot to be making progress: since(T) < stuckPeriod OR d1 - d0 > stuckDist. Action: reset clock T. No trigger.

CD-MV-Beh18 [Functional Requirement] MV_AvoidingAgain_to_GettingOut: In AvoidingAgain, the movement subsystem transitions to GettingOut when stuck detection fires: since(T) >= stuckPeriod AND d1 - d0 <= stuckDist. No trigger, no action.

CD-MV-Beh19 [Functional Requirement] MV_AvoidingAgain_to_Found: In AvoidingAgain, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh20 [Functional Requirement] MV_AvoidingAgain_to_Waiting: In AvoidingAgain, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem transitions to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh21 [Functional Requirement] MV_GettingOut_to_Going: In GettingOut, on receiving the turn event with payload a, the movement subsystem transitions to Going. Trigger: turn ? a. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh22 [Functional Requirement] MV_GettingOut_to_Found: In GettingOut, on receiving the stop event, the movement subsystem transitions to Found. Trigger: stop. No guard, no action.

CD-MV-Beh23 [Functional Requirement] MV_GettingOut_to_Waiting: In GettingOut, on receiving the resume event, the movement subsystem transitions to Waiting. Trigger: resume. No guard, no action.

CD-DC1 [Design Constraint] UniqueTransitions: Each behavioural requirement (CD-GA-Beh1..7 and CD-MV-Beh1..23) maps to exactly one transition. There are no duplicate transitions for the same source state and trigger event combination. Implementations must not collapse or duplicate transitions.
